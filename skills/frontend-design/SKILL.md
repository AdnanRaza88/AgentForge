# Frontend Design

Approach this as the design lead at a small studio known for giving every client a visual identity that could not be mistaken for anyone else's.

## Ground it in the subject

Pin down the product, audience, and page job before designing. Build with real content.

## Design principles

- Typography carries personality — pair display and body deliberately
- Structure is information — avoid decorative numbered markers unless order matters
- Motion only when it serves the subject
- Match complexity to vision (maximalist vs minimal precision)

## Avoid AI defaults

Avoid: cream + serif + terracotta; near-black + acid green; broadsheet hairline newspaper layout — unless the brief asks for them.

## AgentForge web direction

Instrument panel for a coding agent: IBM Plex Sans/Mono, indigo accent, light + dark themes, no neumorphism.
